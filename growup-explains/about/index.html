ABOUT THE PROJECT

Curiosity is not a distraction.
It's the method.

Growup Explains is a small independent learning publication created by a Japan-based educator. The starting point is a question someone genuinely wanted answered.

THE PHILOSOPHY

From “I don't get it” to “Oh, now I see.”

School subjects often arrive as neat rules and formulas. Real understanding usually begins one step earlier: Why is this rule true? What changes if the conditions change? Does this explanation actually make sense?

We want explanations that welcome those questions. Some pieces are about chemistry or mathematics; others may explore technology, games, or everyday mysteries. The common thread is intellectual curiosity rather than a single school subject.

Our editorial approach

We use AI-assisted drafting and editing to help develop this project. We aim to check technical claims, conditions and equations, and to make corrections when errors are found. AI-generated text is never a substitute for a good explanation or subject-matter judgment.

Where something is approximate—like steam's expansion ratio—we say what assumptions make the number meaningful. Where a reaction requires special conditions, we state them.

What comes next?

More free explainers, interactive mini-lessons, and eventually optional downloadable practice material. If a resource is still in development, we label it that way.
